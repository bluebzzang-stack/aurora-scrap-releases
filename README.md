# Aurora Scrap Releases

Aurora Scrap is a Windows and macOS desktop app for saving web pages to your computer and organizing them with categories, labels, and notes. Choose PNG, JPG, PDF, MHTML, or SingleFile HTML to keep research references you can return to.

This is the official release distribution repository maintained by the Aurora Scrap development team. The app's automatic updater uses this repository's Releases for update files and metadata.

## Download and try it

- **Windows:** [Get Aurora Scrap from Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/XP99GV8VFVZ5N5).
- **macOS:** [Download the latest release](https://github.com/bluebzzang-stack/aurora-scrap-releases/releases/latest). Choose the DMG for your Mac's processor.
- **Demo:** [Watch the illustrative web-page saving workflow demo (MP4)](https://scrap.waple.net/guides/assets/aurora-scrap-demo-english.mp4). No sign-in is required to watch. This silent demo illustrates the workflow with an English interface and on-screen explanations.
- **Demo transcript · 대본 · 全文 · 文字稿:** [English](docs/demo/en.md) · [한국어](docs/demo/ko.md) · [日本語](docs/demo/ja.md) · [简体中文](docs/demo/zh.md).
- **Product details:** [Visit the official website](https://scrap.waple.net/?lang=en).

Start free with **2 workspaces, up to 1 GB each**. Paid licenses are sold separately as a one-time purchase for the current major version; see [plans and license terms](https://scrap.waple.net/purchase.php?lang=en) for current details.

Saving results depend on the site's access restrictions, dynamic content, and chosen format. Check the saved text and images, and test offline access before relying on an archive.

## Free offline worksheet kit

[Download the four-language worksheet kit (ZIP)](docs/worksheets/aurora-scrap-worksheet-kit.zip) · [Usage instructions](docs/worksheets/README.txt)

Includes eight plain-text worksheets: one research note and one three-reference comparison worksheet in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Extract the ZIP, open your language folder, and fill in a copy in any text editor. No account or purchase is required. These are manual worksheets, not automatic comparison features. Prepared by the Aurora Scrap development team.

## Compare three references before choosing a design

Use the free comparison worksheet to separate observations, interpretations, and a design decision. Fill it in manually; the app does not automatically compare or score pages.
