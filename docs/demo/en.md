This guide is published by the Aurora Scrap development team.

This silent demo illustrates the saving workflow with an English interface and embedded English explanations. It is an illustrative workflow demo, not a verified recording of a live app session.

[Watch the workflow demo (MP4)](https://scrap.waple.net/guides/assets/aurora-scrap-demo-english.mp4) · [Read the research guide](https://scrap.waple.net/guides/en/)

## Timed transcript

1. **00:00–00:03** Start a new web scrap from the app header.
2. **00:03–00:06** Enter the page URL and review its title.
3. **00:06–00:09** Choose a category, save formats, and capture area.
4. **00:09–00:14** Start saving. The new item appears in your library.
5. **00:14–00:20** Inspect and zoom the saved screenshot.
6. **00:20–00:24** The Media tab lists the media found in this demo.
7. **00:24–00:27** Filter the list by media type.
8. **00:27–00:30** Open Content (MD) to read the saved text.
9. **00:30–00:35** You can also start from the Chrome extension.
10. **00:35–00:38** Choose the capture area and formats, then start saving.
11. **00:38–00:42** Return to the app to review your saved reference.
